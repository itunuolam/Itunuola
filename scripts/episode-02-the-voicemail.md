# THE FINAL CLUE — Episode 02: "The Voicemail"

**Runtime target:** 85 seconds
**Format:** Cinematic photorealistic short, no voice-over, minimal essential dialogue (one line, heard as audio only — not shown as an on-screen speaking character)

---

## CHARACTER BIBLE

**Main Character — Elena Cross**
27-year-old woman, straight shoulder-length black hair usually pulled back in a low ponytail, warm brown eyes, olive skin tone, slim build. Wears a fitted burgundy long-sleeve top, black jeans, and a thin silver ring on her right hand. Calm, introverted, slightly guarded expression that shifts to visible unease as the story progresses.

> Repeat these exact details in every AI image prompt where Elena appears, to preserve visual consistency.

**Naomi Cross (Elena's identical twin, unseen in person until Scene 8)**
Identical face and build to Elena, but paler, thinner, with damp hospital-tousled black hair and a pale blue hospital gown, an IV line taped to her arm. Only shown in a single still photograph in Scene 8 — never in motion, never speaking on camera.

**Recurring Object — The Phone**
A plain black smartphone with a cracked bottom-right corner of the screen, always shown face-up on a nightstand or in Elena's hand, screen glowing.

---

## VISUAL STYLE (apply to every scene)

Photorealistic cinematic film still. Natural human proportions. Realistic skin texture. Dramatic but believable lighting. Shallow depth of field. 35mm cinematic photography. High detail. No cartoon. No anime. No illustration. No text in the image (phone screens should show a soft glow, not legible text).

## QUALITY CHECK

1. Strong first 5 seconds? ✅ Caller ID showing her own name creates instant, specific curiosity.
2. Is there a mystery? ✅ Who is calling, why the voice is identical, and what "the lake house" means.
3. Does the story escalate? ✅ Strange call → traced to unfamiliar hospital → hidden birth certificates → weakening urgent calls → hospital arrival.
4. Unexpected ending? ✅ No supernatural double or hoax — a previously unknown identical twin was trying to warn her about a hereditary heart condition before it was too late.
5. Can every scene be generated visually? ✅ Every scene is one grounded, realistic composition — no fantastical effects needed.
6. Is the main character visually consistent? ✅ Same hair, eyes, skin tone, outfit, and ring repeated in every prompt.
7. Does it avoid clichés? ✅ No evil twin, no jump scare, no "it was a dream" — a sincere, human sacrifice instead.
8. Would someone want to watch until the end? ✅ Escalating dread resolves into genuine emotional payoff, not a gimmick.

## NOTE ON SERIES CONSISTENCY
