## QUALITY CHECK

1. Strong first 5 seconds? ✅ A photo of a future birthday sliding under the door creates instant curiosity.
2. Is there a mystery? ✅ Who is sending the photos, and how do they come true?
3. Does the story escalate? ✅ From odd photos → hospital photo → empty camera footage → hidden mail → disoriented drive → hospital confirmation.
4. Unexpected ending? ✅ No supernatural sender — she is the one taking the photos during blackouts, having received a diagnosis she couldn't consciously face.
5. Can every scene be generated visually? ✅ Each scene is a single grounded, realistic shot with no complex or abstract effects.
6. Is the main character visually consistent? ✅ Same hair, eyes, freckles, cardigan, necklace repeated in every prompt.
7. Does it avoid clichés? ✅ No jump scare, no "it was all a dream," no ghost — psychological/medical grounding instead.
8. Would someone want to watch until the end? ✅ Escalating dread plus a deeply human, emotional payoff.
